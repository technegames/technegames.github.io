Sand Smash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Techne Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Sand Smash (the "app"). We keep it simple: we don't run our own servers, we don't have user accounts, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which does collect some information, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score and your sound setting in a file on your device so they're remembered between sessions. This data never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It's deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, personalize, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device information, such as model, operating system version, and language
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • app performance and diagnostic information

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland): where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • Ad personalization: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google's ad settings: manage how Google personalizes ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. We don't hold any other data about you. For data held by Google, use the tools linked above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

Security

Since we don't collect or store your personal information ourselves, there's no data on our side to secure. Google protects the information it collects according to its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. We'll post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
